# ATH-000 – Athena Philosophy

## Mission
Build an AI-powered Quantitative Research Operating System focused on discovering, validating and explaining robust trading strategies.

## Core Principles
- Research before execution
- Evidence over optimization
- Reproducibility over convenience
- Explainability over black-box decisions
- Plugin-first architecture
- Configuration over hardcoding
- Scientific validation before deployment

## What Athena is NOT
- Just a trading bot
- Just a charting application
- Just a backtester
