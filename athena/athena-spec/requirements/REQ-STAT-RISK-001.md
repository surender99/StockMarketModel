# REQ-STAT-RISK-001

**Requirement ID:** REQ-STAT-RISK-001

**Title:** Risk Analytics

**Purpose:** Compute drawdown, volatility, VaR, CVaR, and tail risk from equity curves.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- [ ] Max drawdown and average drawdown computed
- [ ] Drawdown duration tracked
- [ ] Annualized volatility and downside volatility computed
- [ ] VaR and CVaR at 95% confidence computed
