## Mission

Build an AI-native Quantitative Research Operating System focused on discovering, validating, and explaining robust trading strategies.

## Core Principles

1. Research before execution
2. Evidence over optimization
3. Reproducibility over convenience
4. Explainability over black-box models
5. Plugin-first architecture
6. Configuration over hardcoding
7. Statistical validation before deployment
8. Human approves final trading decisions

Principles 1–7 are defined in [ATH-000-Philosophy.md](../ATH-000-Philosophy.md). Principle 8 is the governance extension for live-capital and production workflows.
